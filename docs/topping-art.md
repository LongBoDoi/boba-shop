# Nine-bin topping station

The layout anchors are the back and front edges in `toppingGeometry.ts`. The intermediate row edges are interpolated from those anchors, keeping the outer sides and both column dividers straight through all three rows. Adjust the end anchors rather than individual row corners; `check-topping-layout.cjs` checks the production divider lines for bends.

The reference's inset 3×3 tray uses three matching transparent atlases, generated with the built-in imagegen tool:

- `src/assets/kitchen/topping-pans.png`: nine filled pans plus an empty pan for ingredients that have not unlocked.
- `src/assets/kitchen/topping-scoops.png`: matching portions carried from the tray to the cup.
- `src/assets/kitchen/topping-pieces.png`: individual pieces inside the drink.

`src/components/art/toppingArt.ts` measures each atlas region. `ToppingSprites.tsx` draws each region through an SVG viewport at render time; the PNGs remain unchanged. The viewport is what does the clipping on purpose: an oversized `<Image>` inside an `overflow: hidden` box is clipped correctly on a phone, but Chrome paints the wrong slice of its background raster for the cells nearest the sheet's left edge, which drew dark pearls as popping boba and red beans as aloe until the element happened to repaint. The cup uses static SVG viewports inside its existing animated settling group and wet-surface mask.

The tray is filled in the catalogue's own order, read like a page: left to right along the back row, then the next row forward — so the week opens pans from the back-left corner outwards. The foreground row widens with the counter perspective. Each pan carries its Vietnamese name on a small cream card clipped to its near lip, where the ingredients never reach; lifting a scoop still names it on the hint line, and every available pan retains its accessible button label.

The pan rows fill the painted basin from its back lip to its front lip. `toppingGeometry.ts` divides one perspective plane into adjoining cells and projects each atlas pan's tapered rims onto its shared boundaries. This closes the triangular gaps that remain when independently tapered sprites are merely placed side by side. A two-pixel rim overlap covers rounded corners and subpixel seams. Filled and locked empty pans share these bounds and their corresponding pickup positions. The fit is not handed to a renderer as a transform: a 4×4 matrix is a projective transform, and a renderer that cannot perform one reduces it to a translate, a rotate and a scale, dropping the skew and the perspective divide — which left a gap between every pan and the next on a phone while a browser looked right. `toppingPanBands` reads the same matrix and returns the cell as a stack of horizontal bands, each a plain rectangle carrying its own slice of the painted cell. The band count comes from how far the rims travel between the back of the cell and the front, so the step between bands stays under half a point at any size.

The user approved three additional toppings. Red beans (`redbean`), aloe jelly (`aloe`) and white pearls (`crystal`) unlock on days 6, 7 and 8. Existing unlock order, two-topping capacity and save format remain compatible. Popping boba and pandan now use the reference's orange and muted green palette in trays, scoops, drink pieces and order icons.

Validation: lint, typecheck and the existing regression suite, extended to cover the new toppings' unlock days, duplicate/capacity rules, save round trips and perfect serving after reload. Browser interaction checks exercised all nine bins at 375×667, 390×844 and 768×1024, plus the early-day tray, including horizontal panning, scoop pickup and cup contents. No native simulator was run.

## Pan atlas prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: a SINGLE transparent sprite atlas for a mobile boba-game topping station. Match the supplied warm hand-painted milk-tea kitchen illustration: honey cream light, caramel brown soft outlines, restrained gouache/watercolor shading, appetizing glossy ingredients, warm silver stainless steel with brown reflections. No pink, no neon, no photorealism, no text or labels, no checkerboard. Real transparent pixels outside the sprites. Exactly TWELVE separate shallow stainless steel ingredient pans in a perfectly regular THREE COLUMN by FOUR ROW grid. Each pan has the SAME shape, viewpoint and size. Seen from a little above and straight in front like a counter inset: wide shallow rounded rectangular opening, broad polished thin silver rim, small beveled brown-shadowed front lip, food lies below rim, no big upright sides. Each complete pan approximately TWO times wider than tall. Keep every pan fully inside its own equally sized cell, with ample transparent gutters between pans and around the canvas. The first NINE pans are filled as follows: Read cells left to right, top to bottom. Row 1: (1) glossy deep dark-brown round tapioca pearls; (2) small translucent milky-white lychee jelly pearls; (3) translucent golden ORANGE round popping boba. Row 2: (4) small glossy russet RED BEANS with an oval bean shape; (5) golden-yellow custard pudding soft little cubes; (6) pale creamy-white translucent ALOE JELLY CUBES. Row 3: (7) muted olive-green pandan jelly soft little cubes; (8) near-black charcoal grass jelly irregular cubes; (9) glossy ivory WHITE CRYSTAL TAPIOCA PEARLS, larger and more opaque than lychee. The FOURTH ROW has THREE identical EMPTY stainless pans, gently warm grey interiors. All ingredients should be full, densely packed and big enough to recognize at small mobile size. No scoops, no spoons, no handles, no lid, no stickers, no wooden plaques, no signs, no tabletop, no surrounding station or counter, no joined trays. These are separate individual bins to place in a tightly inset 3 by 3 counter. Landscape 3:2 atlas.
```
